# CC11 Alpha channel plan

## Goal

Earn serious technical attention for the research programme and recruit useful
critique. The alpha campaign is not a lead-generation funnel and does not claim
general product availability.

## Four-week sequence

| Week | Theme | Primary artefact | Call to action |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The boundary | 30-second landscape film + campaign page | Inspect the model |
| 2 | The mechanism | Six-stage evidence path diagram | Challenge a premise |
| 3 | The implementation | CDD Core, Studio and CourseForge walkthrough | Reproduce a check |
| 4 | The open work | Public release ledger and unresolved gates | Follow or contribute |

## Channel adaptations

### LinkedIn

- One strong claim per post.
- Use the landscape film for the launch post.
- Follow with a document-style sequence built from the six operating stages.
- Put limitations in the post, not in a defensive comment later.
- Route technical discussion to the public repository.

### GitHub

- Pin the alpha campaign and current research snapshot in the README.
- Release notes must link code, protocol, evidence and known limitations to one
  commit.
- Use discussions or issues for falsification attempts and research questions.
- Do not mirror private CC10 issue data.

### YouTube

- Publish the 30-second film as a trailer only after the page is stable.
- Follow with a 4–6 minute narrated walkthrough using real public CDD screens.
- Chapters: premise, model, evidence, demo, open gates.
- Keep comments for substantive questions; do not automate promotional replies.

### Fediverse

- Use the vertical film without sound dependency.
- Include descriptive alt text and the alpha status in the post body.
- Prefer a short thread that exposes the model and open gates.

### cong42.de

- `/cc11/` is the canonical campaign page.
- The CC10 homepage links to it as a research preview, not a replacement for the
  running system.
- The public CDD repository remains the inspection and contribution surface.

## Success signals

No vanity target is invented for the alpha. Record:

- qualified research questions;
- reproducibility reports;
- falsification attempts;
- accepted corrections;
- useful external references;
- conversion from campaign page to public research artefacts.

Any future numeric target must be declared before the measurement period.
